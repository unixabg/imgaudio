imgaudio

Turn a photo into sound, or find the picture hidden in any sound. Then turn the sound into music.

80 Hz800 Hz8 kHz
Starting Python. The first visit downloads about 30 MB; later visits load from cache.

01Start from a photo or a sound

No photo yet

The photo stays on your device. Large photos are shrunk before processing, since the sound only uses a few hundred points across.

No sound yet

Any recording works: a voice memo, birdsong, music, or a WAV saved from this page. Twenty to sixty seconds reads best, so longer sounds are trimmed. The sound stays on your device.

02Pick how it sounds

plain brightness, the honest spectrogram
Each pitch is raised by octaves into visible light and takes that colour, so every sound shares one palette and a note looks the same in every octave. Brightness is loudness.
Reads 40 to 4000 Hz instead of 80 to 8000 Hz. Tick it if everything sits at the bottom of the picture.
More settings
Mixes the original sound back beneath the notes, so its picture partly survives in the music.
Sound carries brightness only. This keeps a tiny colour map beside it so the recovered picture is in colour.

The round trip: your sound became a picture, and the picture became sound again. What you hear is what the picture kept.

The picture, read back out of the music

The music is made of new notes, so this mostly shows the notes. Raise "Picture under the music" in More settings to bring the photo back. Shown in grey because colour would be painted on, not recovered.
